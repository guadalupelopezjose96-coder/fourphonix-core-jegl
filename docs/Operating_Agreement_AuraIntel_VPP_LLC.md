# OPERATING AGREEMENT OF AURAINTEL VPP LLC
**A Single-Member Limited Liability Company**

**THIS OPERATING AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is made and entered into effective as of the date of formation filed with the Department of State, by and between **AURAINTEL VPP LLC** (the "Company") and **José Emanuel Guadalupe López**, as the sole Member and Manager (the "Member").

### ARTICLE I: FORMATION AND PURPOSE
1.1 **Formation.** The Member has formed a Limited Liability Company pursuant to the Limited Liability Company Act of the State of formation.
1.2 **Purpose.** The primary purpose of the Company is to design, architect, develop, license, and operate Virtual Power Plant (VPP) software, Distributed Energy Resource Management Systems (DERMS), automated telemetry, and demand-response optimization services, and to engage in any lawful business activity permitted by law.
1.3 **Regulatory Ring-Fencing.** Unless explicitly licensed as a wholesale market participant or aggregator before the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) or applicable Independent System Operator (ISO/RTO), all commercial SaaS operations shall be strictly construed as software optimization and telemetry processing services, and not as retail public utility electricity sales.

### ARTICLE II: MANAGEMENT AND AUTHORITY
2.1 **Sole Manager.** The Company shall be Manager-managed. **José Emanuel Guadalupe López** is hereby designated as the Chief Executive Officer, Sole Manager, and Authorized Security Officer (ASO) with full, exclusive, and complete authority to:
   (a) Open, maintain, and close corporate bank accounts (including ACH, Wire, and Letters of Credit);
   (b) Execute interconnection agreements, market participant agreements, and NAESB/OATI PKI certificate enrollments;
   (c) Retain 100% ownership of all source code, cryptographic keys, patents, and intellectual property developed by or for the Company.

### ARTICLE III: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNIFICATION
3.1 **Limited Liability.** The debts, obligations, and liabilities of the Company, whether arising in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall be solely the debts, obligations, and liabilities of the Company, and the Member/Manager shall not be obligated personally for any such debt, obligation, or liability.
